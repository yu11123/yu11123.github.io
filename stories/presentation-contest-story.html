6th NTU Presentation Contest / Silver Award

Telling my illness story on stage.

Overview

A question about AI and work led me to a personal one: if our abilities change, what remains of our value as people?

Overview The speech Behind the speech At the contest At a glance

The final-round speech

AI is here.
Love is the answer.

AI 來了，但愛是解答

The title plays on AI and ài (愛), the Mandarin word for love. I used that wordplay to ask whether our value lies only in the work we can do, or also in the care we give one another.

Behind the speech

At thirteen, I was diagnosed with a tumor. I underwent two major surgeries and spent months unable to eat normally. Treatment was painful. During that time, I also received care from my family, friends, and teachers.

In the speech, I reflected on what that care meant to me. Even when I could not rely on the abilities I had taken for granted, I could still receive love and give it to others. I connected that thought to listening to people’s stories as a school counseling-office volunteer and volunteering with The Big Issue.

I began with the possibility of a robot replacing the woman at my neighborhood breakfast shop. By the end, the question was about more than her job: it was about the warmth and care that make an everyday encounter matter.

At the contest

My speech received a Silver Award at the 6th NTU Presentation Contest.
