What keeps me curious.

Music, culture, and exploring at my own pace.

Singer

I sang in my elementary-school choir from six to twelve and joined my junior-high choir at thirteen. I kept singing and have performed onstage every year for the past nine years.

See my performances →

Guitarist

I bought my first guitar after high school. NTU’s guitar club gave me people to form bands with and opportunities to sing and play on my own. I still perform after graduation.

See my performances →

Concertgoer

Live music has become a way I remember different seasons of my life. I attend around 25 concerts a year and sometimes travel to Japan or Singapore for the artists I love.

Japanese Culture

Self-taught to JLPT N1, I can now translate, speak with people in Japanese, and understand more of the culture behind what I enjoy.

Exhibitions

I visit museums and galleries throughout the year, from photography shows and painting exhibitions to natural history collections.

Cycling

On a bike, I can explore at my own pace, moving through the city, noticing the route, and leaving room for an unplanned stop.
